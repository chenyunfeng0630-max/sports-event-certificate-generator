# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Protect participant privacy and use fictional data in public examples.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security advisory flow when privacy is required. The maintainer may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and intentionally kept concise for a small project.
